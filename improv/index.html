Improv

Browse upcoming shows, classes, and performers at improv theaters in New York and beyond. Mark what you're going to, and never miss a show.

Available for iPhone and iPad

Download on the App Store

Every show, one feed

A chronological feed of upcoming shows at your theater, grouped by date. Search by name, browse by night, or filter to find exactly what you want.

Classes and workshops

See what's offered, from introductory workshops to advanced intensives. Tap through to details, schedules, and registration.

Mark what you're going to

Tap "I'm Going" on any show to keep a personal list of your upcoming nights out. Your list stays on your device.

Follow the performers

Look up performers and see everything they're in. Find new shows through the people you already like.

Pick your city and theater

Switch between cities and theaters from the sidebar. The app remembers your last selection and picks up where you left off.

And nothing else

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Improv fetches show data and that's it.
