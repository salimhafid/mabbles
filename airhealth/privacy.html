Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

AirHealth shows your sleep and recovery using data your Fitbit Air records. The short version: your health data stays on your iPhone, and AirHealth collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers. The long version follows.

What AirHealth reads

With your permission, AirHealth reads these from Apple Health, and only from the sources you choose, normally the Google Health app:

  • Sleep, including sleep stages
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, and respiratory rate
  • Blood oxygen
  • Steps, distance, flights climbed, active energy, VO₂ max, and workouts

AirHealth only reads. It never writes to or changes anything in Apple Health. You can turn off its access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

What AirHealth stores, and where

AirHealth keeps daily summaries worked out from that data, along with any tags you log (like caffeine or a late meal), in its own storage on your iPhone. Its widgets read the same storage. The storage is protected by your iPhone's passcode encryption and is not synced to iCloud. Deleting the app deletes it.

Summaries of your nights appear in Spotlight search on your iPhone. The morning note is written either from a template or, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, by Apple's on-device model. Either way, it's written on your iPhone.

What the developer receives

Nothing. AirHealth contains no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party code. The developer cannot see your health data, your tags, or that you use the app at all. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. If you email support, the developer receives your email. That is the complete list.

Network connections AirHealth makes

None. AirHealth works entirely on your iPhone. If a future version ever needs to connect to a service, this policy will say so, with a new effective date, before that version ships.

Health data is never used for anything else

Your health data is used only to show you your own sleep and recovery. It is never sold, never shared, never used for advertising, and never leaves your device.

Children

AirHealth does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included. It does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date.
