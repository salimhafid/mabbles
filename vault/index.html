OmniVault X

Your passwords, encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, stored in your own iCloud. No server. No account. No tracking.

Available for Mac, iPhone, and iPad

Download on the App Store Download on the Mac App Store

Encrypted before it leaves

Every item is encrypted on your device before it is stored or synced. Your passphrase is the only key. OmniVault X never stores it, never sends it, and cannot reset it.

No server, no account

Vaults sync through your own iCloud account, and what travels is ciphertext. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to be breached.

Unlock the way you like

Touch ID or Face ID unlocks your key, released only by the Secure Enclave after a live match. Your passphrase still governs, and is the only way in on a new device.

Fill where you work

AutoFill on iPhone and iPad. A menu bar panel on the Mac. A Chrome extension that asks the app, never the other way round.

Everything in one place

Logins, cards, identities, secure notes, SSH keys, passkeys, and one-time codes. A password generator with an honest strength estimate.

Built to be checked

No third-party code. No analytics. No crash reporters. The cryptography is standard and pinned to published test vectors.
